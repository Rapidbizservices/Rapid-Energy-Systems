Rapid Energy Systems (RES) is a California-based developer focused on utility-grade battery energy storage projects that deliver real grid value—capacity, reliability, and flexibility. Our flagship project is a 9.6 MW / 19.3 MWh Tesla Megapack BESS in Calipatria, CA.

About Rapid Energy Systems

Rapid Energy Systems (RES) specializes in standalone battery energy storage assets using bankable, Tier‑1 technology. We focus on high‑impact projects in the ~10 MW range that utilities take seriously and structure investments to maximize tax efficiency through ITC, bonus depreciation, and Energy Community incentives.

Flagship Project: 9.6 MW Tesla Megapack BESS (Calipatria, CA)

Location: 340 Wilkinson Rd, Calipatria, CA 92233

Technology: Tesla Megapack 2 XL

Size: 9.6 MW / 19.3 MWh (2‑hour duration)

Grid: Imperial Irrigation District (IID) region

Status: Site identified, project plan prepared, interconnection path targeted.

This single‑phase, utility‑grade BESS asset is designed to provide Resource Adequacy (RA), support peak shaving and capacity markets, and deliver fast‑response grid services such as frequency and voltage support.
